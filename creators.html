The creators of the acclaimed Cube Escape series, Rusty Lake Hotel, Roots, Paradise, The White Door, The Past Within, Underground Blossom and most recently The Mr. Rabbit Magic Show!
Rusty Lake is an indie game studio based in Amsterdam, founded by Robin Ras and Maarten Looise. Since spring 2015, we have developed 19 surrealistic adventure games set in our mysterious, interconnected universe. Inspired by David Lynch’s Twin Peaks, we’ve crafted an eerie, puzzle-filled world where every game reveals deeper secrets of Rusty Lake.
Our journey began with 10 free Cube Escape episodes, followed by premium titles like Rusty Lake Hotel, Rusty Lake: Roots, and Rusty Lake Paradise. In 2020, we launched our publishing label Second Maze, releasing The White Door, Milo and the Magpies, Mitoza, Milo and the Christmas Gift, and Follow the Meaning.
Meanwhile, Rusty Lake expanded with our first co-op game, The Past Within (2022), later launching on Nintendo Switch, and Underground Blossom (2023), a haunting metro adventure which explores the life of Laura Vanderboom. We also revisited our origins with Cube Escape Collection, a remastered bundle of our first 9 Cube Escape chapters.
Another one of our ambitious projects is Cube Escape: Paradox - a short film, game, and comic crossover that was funded by our incredible community in just 9 hours on Kickstarter.
The heart of Rusty Lake lies in our fans: the theorists, the artists, and even those with Rusty Lake tattoos. Your passion (and stunning #rustylake fan arts, which decorate most areas of our office as well as the feed of our social media) keeps us inspired and driven to create more adventures that you all can enjoy! For a chance to get featured in our regular #FanArtFriday posts, make sure to tag your fan art with #rustylake on social media, and if you’d like to mail us your fan art directly, you can find our office address at the bottom of the About Section! Thank you for being part of our journey!
